## Abstract
We discuss the status of follow-up studies of magnetic O-star candidates identified by MiMeS. We confirm a magnetic field detection for the O-type bright giant 63 Oph and present an analysis of the star's polarimetric, spectroscopic, and photometric variability. Finally, we discuss the implications of this detection for our understanding of magnetic field evolution in massive stars.
